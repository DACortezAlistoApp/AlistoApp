Why people trust Alisto

Safety and clarity, not just convenience

Every booking is matched with verified people, clear terms, and tools that keep both sides protected.

Verified contractors & riders

People on the platform go through identity and document checks. For mobility, each rider is linked to one authorized motorcycle so you always know who’s arriving.

Live tracking & clear pricing

See where your helper or rider is in real time. Callout fees, bids, and totals are shown before you confirm — no surprise charges after the job.

Privacy you control

Your location is only shared while a job is active. You review and accept the privacy terms before your first booking, and can manage consent anytime.
